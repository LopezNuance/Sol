Date: 2026-09-08 (UTC)

`docs/decisions/2026-09-07-phase0-venue.md`)
Subject: promoted `Sol_RFC.md` (v0.1.0), regenerated docx export, published
schemas, corpus + validator, demonstration evidence

## Scope

Per the Phase 0 venue decision, the internal stage requires a review/comment
cycle on the promoted version with every finding triaged into exactly one
bucket: **errata** (spec wording), **corpus entry** (new pathology, no spec
change), or **unfreeze candidate** (batched, never per-item).

## Method

1. Cross-reference check of all section references introduced by the
   promotion (§14.6, §15, §16.4, §18, §20, §23, §24, §41, §42, §43, §48,
   §53, §59, Appendix A, Appendix C).
2. Vocabulary consistency: new normative text checked against the registered
   vocabularies (§17.2 autonomy levels vs. actor schema enum; §20.1 status
   values; §43 codec identifiers; §24.2 availability values).
3. Examples-must-validate gate (Change Control): every touched example
   checked against the published schemas (§15 manifest, §16.4 agent, §18
   cell, §21 base record, §23 claim, §24.1/24.2 evidence, §42 object).
4. Appendix B vs. corpus: all 37 `expected_diagnostics.json` files compared
   line-by-line with the Appendix B manifest.
5. docx staleness probes against the regenerated export.
6. Full validation suite re-run on the promoted tree.
